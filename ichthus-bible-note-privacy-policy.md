# Privacy Policy for Ichthus Bible + Note

**Last Updated: May 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how stackedCG handles your information when you use our application.

## 1. Local-First Data Storage
The application is designed to be "local-first." All data you create (notes, database entries, settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

## 2. No Data Collection
We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage statistics, or crash reports to any external servers.

## 3. Permissions
The application may request the following permissions:
* **Storage/File Access:** To save and load your local database files.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The application does not use any third-party services or analytics.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have questions about this policy, please reach out to us at stackedCG@gmail.com or via our GitHub repository.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
This application does not collect any personal information from children or any other users.
